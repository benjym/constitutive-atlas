Is the effect larger than the numerical change?

Both initialization scenarios were checked at maximum axial increments 0.0002, 0.0001 and 0.00005. Adjacent changes must decrease under the frozen numerical rule. This checks output sensitivity over those increments, not physical accuracy or constitutive equations.
