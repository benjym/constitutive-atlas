## Two separate control problems

| Proposed track | Supplied controls | Predictions | Gate before execution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stress-controlled cyclic response | Reviewed shear-stress history and documented normal/height/volume controls | Shear strain history, residual strain, stiffness evolution and admissible normal/effective-stress response | Apparatus/control interpretation, full initial state and stable mixed-control solve |
| Strain-conditioned replay | Measured shear-strain path and documented normal constraints | Shear-stress loops and other unsupplied responses | Explicit declaration that evaluation strain is conditioning information; appropriate history initialization |

Keep the tracks separate. Replaying measured strain cannot establish stress-controlled strain prediction. Source axial/normal stresses and excess-pressure channels are not triaxial p and q. The DSS source explicitly omits saturation and uses constant-height control; its pressure channel must therefore not be presented as a directly measured saturated pore-water response. The supplied visualization script contains inconsistent axial/shear and mean/vertical labels. A subsequent [source-column identity audit](dss_pressure_identity_audit.json) verifies that all 17 files are consistent with pressure = per-file reference minus current vertical stress within printed rounding. Use the inferred per-file reference rather than nominal 40/100 kPa; reference timing and apparatus corrections remain unresolved. These two channels are algebraically dependent and must not be counted as independent prediction objectives. Constant-height, constant-volume and undrained conditions require apparatus and saturation evidence; zero-filled axial strain is not proof of a measured zero-volume response. Do not equate a normal-stress change to pore pressure until the source convention and boundary conditions justify that observation map.

Strain percentages convert to dimensionless strain and kPa to Pa. Engineering shear requires the tensor/Mandel conversion used by the solver, with the declared sign and plane. A shear test also needs normal-stress/strain constraints and initial lateral stresses; vertical confinement alone does not determine the full effective stress tensor. Torsion needs reviewed geometry, torque-to-stress and rotation-to-strain relations, stress nonuniformity and drainage. Apparatus channels must not be mixed with workbook-corrected specimen quantities without a transformation record.

The cycle coordinate is acquisition/phase information, not physical elapsed seconds. Preserve order, reversal points and partial final cycles. Do not create time-dependent conclusions from a rate-independent replay or infer a waveform between sparse cycle summaries.

## Calibration packages and mechanism comparisons

1. **Material characterization:** Ottawa-specific index, compression and monotonic shear evidence, plus preparation and initial state. Identify this evidence before fitting backbone parameters. The current two cyclic campaigns alone do not establish that the required characterization programme exists.
2. **Limited cyclic information:** selected whole development tests at one reviewed state and amplitude range. Any supplied initial cycles or specimen adaptation count as calibration/conditioning, including memory-state estimation.
3. **Expanded cyclic information:** additional amplitudes and, if state metadata supports it, pressure or density coverage. Use nested packages within the same material and reviewed specimen groups.

Candidate formulations are VW96, IGS97 and a reviewed SANISAND variant, with simple baselines where their domain permits. These are candidates only. HCA would require a separate accumulation/observed-amplitude workflow and is not directly interchangeable with instantaneous loop prediction. PM4Sand dimensional/version review remains separate. Do not treat two SANISAND reset policies as two physical mechanisms or claim an IGS parent reduction from equal stiffness multipliers.

## Outcomes to settle on development evidence

Report per-test shear-strain and shear-stress errors only for unsupplied channels, alongside physical units and completion. Separate loop shape, residual-strain accumulation and stiffness evolution. Loop energy requires complete aligned stress/strain cycles and an approved convention; avoid adjacent-row train/test splits. A stress-controlled and strain-conditioned score cannot share an unqualified ranking.

For threshold outcomes, freeze the strain definition (single/double amplitude), threshold, crossing interpolation, admissible strain interval and censoring treatment after development review and before final response analysis. Do not assign a universal liquefaction threshold from a column name. Pore-pressure ratio requires verified reference effective stress and a justified pressure observable. A test that ends before crossing contributes a censored outcome rather than an invented failure cycle. Small-strain material-point limitations may set a stricter window than the apparatus failure criterion.

Metrics and weights must be fixed across models before final evaluation. Use cycle/phase-aware comparisons; keep specimens as the uncertainty units and source-derived summaries as dependent observations. Track numerical refinement, mixed-control residuals, history continuity, resets and failures separately from physical errors. Common calibration information and source-specific recommended programmes are distinct analyses.
