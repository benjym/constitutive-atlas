    - heading "Initial state and loading" [level=2] [ref=e95]
    - paragraph [ref=e96]: Published nominal initial state, not measured first-row values. Compression-positive stress/strain/history; symmetric tensors use Mandel order xx, yy, zz, √2xy, √2yz, √2xz. Stress is in Pa; void ratio and history strain are dimensionless. The history is initialized directly; no preparation replay is inferred.

    - heading "What the result establishes" [level=2] [ref=e101]
    - paragraph [ref=e102]: All three paths pass the frozen publication-precision and adjacent-refinement gates. The downloadable summary retains every gate and aggregate experimental error. These are calibration-campaign comparisons, not held-out validation. Historical strain conversion, original-code equivalence, finite-deformation validity and scientific admission remain open. This native research calculation does not extend the interactive laboratory's 10% limit.
