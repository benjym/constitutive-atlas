Accept only through normal final endpoint checks. Nonfinite nextafter results fail; no direct use of constraint values, material branches, history formulas, parameter clamps or modified tolerances is permitted.

Its admissibility rests on the same declared residual tolerance and exact physical constraints used for every other accepted root.
