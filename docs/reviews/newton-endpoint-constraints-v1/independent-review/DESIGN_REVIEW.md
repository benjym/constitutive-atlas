# Independent Newton endpoint-constraint design review

Reviewer: actual independent run `/root/spectral_multiplicity_review`,2026-11-01. Decision: **accept the proposed generic design for prospective qualification**, subject to an explicit documented compatibility change and the gates below. This is neither implementation/backend acceptance nor Duncan E5 approval.

The hydrostatic departure witness is sound. At `pI`, selected maximum and minimum rows are each `tr(H)/3`; their difference is zero. With zero max-history, the first Newton history correction is therefore zero, while anisotropic strain drives a stress direction with nonzero deviator. Every positive line-search fraction has a positive principal range and unchanged zero history. Applying endpoint `h>=D` feasibility to each such iterate rejects all positive fractions, regardless of subdivision. This is a structural impediment, not a source-domain defect or insufficient iteration budget.

The proposed single deletion separates mathematical residual domain from converged physical-state constraints. Every accepted trial still must be finite, satisfy successful residual/domain evaluation, have finite residual and decrease the scaled residual strictly. Initial/reset iterates, converged roots and final integration commits retain physical admissibility. The existing transaction boundary keeps the external parent unchanged after an exhausted solve. A transient out-of-solution-set iterate is not exposed as an accepted material state. Any restriction needed to evaluate the constitutive expression remains a `domain` guard and is not bypassed.

The exact scalar analogue in `check_witness.py` illustrates the distinction without importing production code. Its first full Newton step is not strict descent; the half step is, and the following Newton step reaches the feasible root. Therefore deleting trial feasibility enables a descent route but does not guarantee every full Newton step succeeds or prove convergence for Duncan. Final infeasible-root rejection must still be tested independently.

Compatibility matters: current `docs/SOLUTION_CONSTRAINTS.md` explicitly describes a feasible-iterate Newton policy and backtracking candidate checks. This change intentionally replaces that solver policy. Preserve the prior document identity and update the current documented contract or identify an explicit prospective override; do not claim all prior feature semantics are unchanged. Models relying on a genuine mathematical trial-domain restriction must declare it in `domain`, as that document already distinguishes.

Required prospective gates: exact candidate/source closure and independent budgets before observations; a synthetic feasible root whose Newton path needs an infeasible trial; infeasible roots and invalid initial states still rejected with complete parent rollback; finite/domain/non-descent trial rejection; forced iteration/substep exhaustion, recovery and restart; unchanged actual Duncan source-compliance budgets; and the original Tresca solution-constraints-v4 corpus or an equally exact retained regression of its feasibility/rollback behavior, plus existing solver tests. Native then Wasm must run serially. All outcomes retain their actual identities; no postfailure relaxation of numerical or endpoint gates.
