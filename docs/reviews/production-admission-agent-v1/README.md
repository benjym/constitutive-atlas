## Findings, ordered by consequence

1. **Release and scientific gates remain open for all formulations.** Baseline preflight success checks intact bindings, not scientific acceptance. Crosswalks preserve old v0.1 identities and do not enforce v0.3 initialization on an old low-level binary. A new definition/build needs new artifact-bound qualification. The barodesy historical/current mathematical bridge is exact for parameters, derived expressions, domain, equations and state layout, but cannot attest current binary stress trajectories. Current preview checks and historical full-stress references have different identities and scopes. Do not combine them into an unstated current-build pass.

2. **Terra Cotta and SANISAND blockers cannot be closed by numerical repetition.** The Terra Cotta author image and static code disagree on halved temperature in mixed control and general nonzero-temperature initialization. Complete journal pages and inequalities are absent. SANISAND original p631 delegates loading-process updates to Dafalias (1986), whose readable original is absent. Parent trial-direction resets and cone-normal loading-process rules cannot be assumed equivalent. The closure packets appropriately keep these open; there is no reason to repeat their completed numerical campaigns or create a generic solver extension from these gaps.

3. **Exact unchanged-state zero loading needs a precise admissibility qualification for plasticity.** DP/MCC initialize with scaled yield tolerance `F<=1e-8`. A parent with `F=5e-9` is admitted by that check, but at unchanged stress/state and `gamma=0`, the Fischer–Burmeister residual is `|F|+F=1e-8`, not zero. Therefore the exact algebraic no-drift statement applies to exactly yield-admissible parents (`F<=0`); tolerance-admitted exterior parents have only a tolerance-level interpretation. This is a mathematical caveat, not a newly observed solver failure. The review does not recommend tightening gates after observation. A release claiming literal no drift for all accepted inputs must prospectively specify/test a policy.

4. **MCC actual-partition agreement is useful and limited.** The saved yield-crossing state uses two accepted half steps. Its full-step reference pressure differs by **2711.38581 Pa**; the original discrepancy remains explicitly failed/inapplicable, not discarded. Independent recomputation of reported continuum refinement gives minimum orders **1.00190** (elastic) and **0.986365** (plastic), final relative errors **1.23815e-4** and **3.96431e-5**, inside the existing gates. These two isotropic continuum checks are distinct from 3D discrete residual agreement and cannot prove adaptive branch selection/accuracy throughout the parameter boxes.

5. **Barodesy source selection is defensible, not complete-source equivalence.** Direct visual inspection confirms body Eq8 and Appendix2 Eq32 use `2p/sigma_star`; Appendix3 Eq41 implies `6p/sigma_star`. Fresh scalar NCL reductions recover `1/lambda_star` for the body for all three complete Table2 soils. The appendix interpretation gives materially different slopes, even opposite signs for London/Dresden. The source prints repeated `WT` products in the objective conversion. Zero spin avoids that unresolved convention. Exact zero/hydro branches, SPD interior floors and `abs(a)<=64` are stated numerical support restrictions. A preparation recipe and accepted source-to-release applicability still need their own deliverables.

6. **Terra Cotta refined accuracy is real but expensive.** I independently assessed each saved phase/channel error against its original bound for both backends and all three paths: maximum fraction **0.5860093719**. The failed parent study stays failed. The retained 30 s runs cost **86,098–92,404 substeps**, consuming 86–92% of the unchanged 100,000 cap. The evidence does not establish interactive defaults or model-specific public-host failure/restart behavior. Correct stress hold carries its own preparation, captures stress using incoming rate, preserves the clock and distinguishes 23 engine observations from 22 reference observations.

7. **Retained DP profile failure remains material to claims.** The local Release-Wasm 83-case report is separate later evidence, including persistent-module recovery, while native recovery used separate CLI calls. The earlier Debug-Wasm memory trap has no established cause or repair. Release results cannot be called a Debug pass. Hosted behavior was not reviewed here.
