# Catalogue-wide ensemble test selection — 3 November 2026

The explorer now reads the normal public experiment catalogue rather than fixing the experiment to OPARA-TXCD-100. All 994 records are selectable, with material/ID search, test-type and replay-availability filters. Twelve records have complete, explicit effective-stress triaxial loading/state contracts: six drained and six undrained tests. The other 982 records remain available as measurement/source-channel views, with specific replay gaps. This does not claim that every archived test is executable.

Replay retains every source observation in acquisition order, measured starting effective stress and void ratio, displayed model-history assumptions, exact catalogue definitions and two independent Web Workers. Drained paths impose acquired axial strain and measured radial effective stress (the existing optional constant-confinement control is preserved). Undrained paths impose axial strain, zero volumetric strain and equal lateral effective stresses. Measured effective radial stress and pore pressure are not imposed on the undrained material law. Pore-pressure change is inferred from measured cell-pressure change minus predicted lateral effective-stress change, under the disclosed saturated/incompressible idealization. Effective-stress-path and pore-pressure charts supplement the stress/volume plots.

Records without explicit effective stress are not promoted from reported total/unspecified stresses. Native source channels retain their original units and selectable axes; summaries use markers rather than implying a continuous loading history. Starting-state, solver, applicability and uncalibrated-parameter limitations remain visible. No physical-time, consolidation, experimental calibration or scientific admission claim is added.

Saved studies identify their selected test and exact source. Import loads that test before validation and replay, including from a source-only view or with an active search excluding the imported ID. Changing tests cancels active work, clears previous outcomes and suppresses stale asynchronous loads. Existing illustrative parameter edits are preserved when switching tests. Model initial stress/density are always taken from the newly selected specimen. MCC initialization checks now use that specimen's p and q rather than a hardcoded 103.72 kPa.
