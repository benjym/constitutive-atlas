## Equation reasoning

Let N = n-(n:r)I/3. On the source yield cone, Eq14 gives unit deviatoric n. From Eq21, ds=2G de-<L>2G Rdev and dp=K d_eps_v-<L>K D. Substitution in Eq20's preceding identity Kp L=n:ds-(n:r)dp, on active loading, gives

```
L = [2G(n:de)-K(n:r)d_eps_v]
    / [Kp+2G(B-C tr(n^3))-K D(n:r)].
```

Each numerator term has pressure units; the printed second term alone does not. This is more than a dimensional guess: it follows directly from two displayed uneliminated source equations. Constant-volume tests make the disputed term zero, so their success cannot adjudicate mixed-volume implementation. The candidate stress residual follows Eq21 and avoids separately evaluating the misprinted formula.

For d=(alpha-alpha_in):n>0, b=alpha_b-alpha, Hbar=(2/3)p b0(b:n), E=2G(B-C tr(n^3))-KD(n:r), A=2G(n:de)-K(n:r)d_eps_v, Eqs23-24 give h=b0/d and Kp=Hbar/d. Thus q=A/(Hbar+dE), <L>=d max(q,0), and <L>h=b0 max(q,0). Multiplying before taking the limit is essential. At d->0+, Hbar>0, stress has elastic instantaneous rate, plastic strain/fabric rates vanish, while d_alpha=(2/3)b0 max(A/Hbar,0)b remains finite. Under outward loading p n:d_alpha=A, cancelling N:Ce:d_eps in first-order yield consistency. At A=0 a unique smooth derivative is not established. At d=Hbar=0 different approaches need not agree; no universal continuation follows. Positive-distance softening (negative Kp) is allowed by source p631 and is not repaired by forcing Hbar positive there. A zero loading denominator remains singular.

The endpoint gamma=Delta_lambda*h rearrangement is exactly algebraic for finite d>0,b0>0. Its evaluability at d=0 does not prove the finite-step system has the unique limit root, nor that a gamma/F complementarity condition selects every original singular branch. The current initial guard is conservative support exclusion, not proof for all later trial states. Exponential void-ratio closure integrates the declared constant-solid-volume differential closure for the imposed straight increment; that closure is an explicit project inference. The source is hypoelastic (p623), so no stored-energy, physical-time or finite-rotation claim follows.
