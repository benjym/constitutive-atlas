        - generic [ref=f3e337]:
          - paragraph [ref=f3e338]: A sand plasticity model in which density relative to critical state controls strength and volume change during shearing.
          - paragraph [ref=f3e339]: The model compares the current void ratio with the critical void ratio at the same mean effective stress. This difference, called the state parameter, connects loose and dense sand responses within one formulation. A translating yield cone records directional plastic loading, while bounding and dilatancy surfaces control hardening and volume change.
          - generic [ref=f3e340]:
            - heading "What it describes" [level=3] [ref=f3e341]
            - generic [ref=f3e342]:
              - term [ref=f3e343]: Density and volume change
              - definition [ref=f3e344]: The state parameter influences whether plastic shearing contracts or dilates the material. Changes in pressure also move the critical void ratio, so density alone does not determine the response.
              - term [ref=f3e345]: Pressure-dependent stiffness
              - definition [ref=f3e346]: The elastic shear and bulk moduli increase with mean effective stress through a pressure power law.
              - term [ref=f3e347]: Evolving directional strength
              - definition [ref=f3e348]: A deviatoric back-stress-ratio tensor translates the yield cone during plastic loading. The distance to the bounding surface controls its evolution and can permit softening.
          - generic [ref=f3e349]:
            - heading "Explore drainage during axial compression" [level=3] [ref=f3e350]
            - paragraph [ref=f3e351]: Choose Drained triaxial and Monotonic ramp.             - heading "Starting state" [level=3] [ref=f3e432]
            - paragraph [ref=f3e433]: Supply the effective stress, void ratio e and all six components of the symmetric deviatoric tensor alpha. Its trace must be zero. The illustrative isotropic state uses alpha = 0; this is not a reconstruction of specimen preparation. Initial stress must lie inside or on the yield cone, pressure and void ratio must be positive, and the hardening-distance domain must hold. The parameter e0 stores the initial reference void ratio and remains fixed as current e evolves. Tensor entries use Mandel order xx, yy, zz, √2xy, √2yz, √2xz.
          - generic [ref=f3e434]:
            - heading "Scope and limitations" [level=3] [ref=f3e435]
            - paragraph [ref=f3e436]: "This is the original 1997 constant-m formulation using the first published reference-distance option. Atlas integrates it with backward Euler at small strain in a fixed frame, using compression-positive effective stress. It is rate independent: elapsed time alone does not cause creep or relaxation. The exposed examples use drained or undrained axial strain control."
            - paragraph [ref=f3e437]: This variant excludes the optional evolving yield-width law and the later 2004 fabric formulation. It requires positive mean effective stress and supplies no tension extension, finite-rotation law or fluid transport. Oedometer loading, axial stress control and full plastic cycles are outside the exposed qualification scope. A successful example does not establish accuracy for every parameter or loading path; finite-step error needs resolution checks.
