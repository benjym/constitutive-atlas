## Independent derivation and checks

With strain held, s_dot = -3G lambda s/q, so q_dot = -3G lambda. Setting u = q/Y - 1 yields u_dot = -(3GA/Y)u^n above yield. Separating variables gives u(t)=u0 exp(-ct) for n=1 and [u0^(1-n)+(n-1)ct]^(1/(1-n)) for n>1, c=3GA/Y. The direction of s and total strain remain fixed. Permanent-strain increment is (q0-q)s0/(2Gq0), and alpha increment is (q0-q)/(3G). At/below yield, the hold is stationary. The approach to yield is asymptotic for admitted n; no finite-time yield crossing/projection is implied. At fixed stress the source rate and flow direction are constant, giving linear permanent and total strain increments with unchanged elastic strain. Mean stress stays constant in strain relaxation because permanent flow is traceless. Dissipation is sigma:eps_vp_dot=q lambda>=0. At n=1 the Bingham equivalent viscosity is Y/A for this normalization, not an independent constant when Y changes at fixed A.
