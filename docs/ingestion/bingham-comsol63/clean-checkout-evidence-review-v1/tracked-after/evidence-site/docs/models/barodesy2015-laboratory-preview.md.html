# Barodesy sand2015 draft laboratory preview

[The local runnable definition](../../models/barodesy-sand.json) is a schema v0.3 **draft** of Kolymbas2015 Eq31, [primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1680/geot.14.P.151). The selected law uses fixed spatial components at zero spin, compression-positive effective Cauchy stress and orthonormal Mandel infinitesimal strain-path increments. Six persistent stress components and scalar invariant void ratio are supplied explicitly; literal initial values are examples. This is a named formulation, not a claim of coverage of all barodesy variants.

Default parameters come from the separately attributed Kolymbas/Bathaeian2019 author manuscript Table1, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/nag.2984), including conversion of c4=465kPa to465000Pa. They are illustrative fixtures, not a reproduction of2015 experiments or a Wichtmann calibration. The original2015 source receipt, source/body locators, independent Cartesian oracle and remaining source restrictions are linked in the [ingestion dossier](../ingestion/barodesy-sand/README.md). Source PDFs remain private.

The numerical domain selects a conservative positive-definite, nonzero-stress interior, a density margin above emin, bounded stress/loading normalization and bounded matrix exponential. An ill-conditioned otherwise positive tensor may reject. Exact zero-loading continuation is an explicitly selected extension; zero-stress evolution, zero-loading update tangents and finite spin remain unresolved. No stress floor, density projection or parameter fitting is introduced.

The [instantaneous-rate study](../../catalogue/investigations/barodesy2015-rates-v2/RESULTS.md) passes38 independent Cartesian/high-precision or exact rational source-rate cases and16 rejection/recovery pairs on native/Wasm. Original negative cancellation evidence is preserved separately. The separately identified preview keeps its equations, derived values, parameters and domain unchanged; metadata and explicit formulation identity differ.

The [finite-path preview study](../../catalogue/investigations/barodesy2015-preview-v1/RESULTS.md) exercises all five controls monotonically/cyclically, browser-default1% drained loading, exact zero-update, domain/omitted-state rejection and recovery using production C ABI/CLI. All20 calls/backend and835caller points/backend pass prospectively frozen independent volume/control and backend parity gates. These bounded software checks support a provisional laboratory preview; they do not certify arbitrary stress trajectories or experimental applicability.

Local runnable definitions increase from six to seven; the discovery catalogue stays32 entries. Scientific admission, physical preparation, source-matched calibration, experiments and deployment remain separate open gates. The hosted site remains unchanged until an authorized deployment.
