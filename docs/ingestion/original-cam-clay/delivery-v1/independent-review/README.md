## Findings

1. **The mathematical law and loading interface must be separated.** At fixed p and packing, any interior q satisfying `abs(q) < -M*p*ln(p/pc)` has zero recoverable distortion and the same volumetric recovery. Specifying zero distortion therefore cannot determine q. This is physical underdetermination under those controls, not a missing q evolution equation that may be invented. Source stress probes do determine q by the applied load. A rule retaining old q on strain-only holds is an explicit numerical selection, not a source-derived inverse law.

2. **No new scalar normal-cone primitive is demonstrated.** In axisymmetry `abs(q)+h <= 0` equals the two smooth constraints `f_plus=q+h <= 0`, `f_minus=-q+h <= 0`, where `h=M*p*ln(p/pc)`. Away from the vertex one face is active. At the vertex their normals are `(M,+1)` and `(M,-1)`, and nonnegative combinations yield `Ev_plastic=M*(a_plus+a_minus)` and `Es=a_plus-a_minus`, including isotropic compaction. Scalar `sqrt`, arithmetic, log, transient unknowns and complementarity residual expressions already exist in the DSL. This is an algebraic representation argument, not evidence of Newton convergence or complete material support. It avoids norm division at q=0 without smoothing the source surface.

3. **Corner dissipation needs an explicit source exception.** At a mixed corner normal, `p*Ev_plastic = M*p*(a_plus+a_minus)` whereas extrapolating the smooth formula gives `M*p*abs(Es)=M*p*abs(a_plus-a_minus)`. These differ if both multipliers are positive. Source p97 restricts its displayed 6.10 bis to nonzero distortion, and p101 explicitly broadens permissible directions at the corner and gives virgin volumetric compression. Do not impose the smooth dissipation equality as a global corner acceptance gate or call two-face algebra an everywhere equivalent extension of that equality. The corner rule is source-supported separately; its power interpretation and numerical selection must be stated explicitly.

4. **The existing plastic-multiplier predictor is genuinely unsuitable without further design.** `cpp/include/atlas/solver.hpp:implicit_step` replaces every multiplier residual by the multiplier during prediction and attempts that solve before complementarity. Under rigid recoverable shear, prescribed nonzero distortion cannot be met with zero multipliers. With zero distortion, dq remains undetermined. The new exact-rational rank check gives rank4 for five scaled local unknowns and rank5 after adding a nonzero-distortion right-hand side. This obstruction cannot be fixed by pressure-domain tolerances. The existing `increment_unknown` role avoids the special zero-multiplier predictor, so it would be premature to require a new predictor implementation; active-set initialization, rank and nonsmooth Jacobians still need deliberate tests.

5. **A norm/FB expression is not a semismooth solver guarantee.** Current `asqrt(Jet)` selects zero derivative at zero and `aabs(Jet)` selects the positive branch at zero. The generic Newton solver rejects singular linear systems. A two-face FB equation at zero multiplier/zero slack can still be singular with the current derivative choice. Conversely, an identified active-set or complementarity formulation may fit existing algebra. No compiled experiment was run, so neither success nor necessary core changes are claimed.

6. **Schema profile mismatch is real and narrow.** The current contract permits `axisymmetric_x` only for physical-time ODEs; strain-path residuals bind the first six components of persistent symmetric `sigma` under `small_strain_3d`. Relabeling an axisymmetric rate-independent system as a physical-time ODE is invalid. A source-complete restricted axisymmetric profile or an explicitly reviewed 3D extension/control contract is needed before claiming ordinary public support. Absence of a source return-map algorithm, adaptive tolerances or tangent formula is not itself a missing constitutive equation: those may be declared project numerical choices and qualified later.
